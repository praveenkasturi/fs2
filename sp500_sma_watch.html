AVOID

Do: skip. Downtrend, near death, or death cross. Rallies into the 50 often fail. An old 50-dip from months ago is not a bargain.

RECLAIM

Do: watch, don’t buy. Still under both lines, climbing into the 50 from below. Wait for a daily close back above the 50. Then it can move to WAIT.

WAIT

Do: sit on hands. Repairing or mixed — price already ran, or it is stuck between the two averages. No clean 50-dip yet.

WATCH

Do: put it on a list. Near a golden cross. 50 still under 200, gap ≤ 2%, rising. Not a buy — wait for the actual cross, then a dip to the 50.

HOLD

Do: don’t chase. Uptrend is already on, or it just golden-crossed. If you own it, you can hold. New money waits for the next dip to the 50.

BUY DIP

Do: check the daily, then maybe buy. Uptrend, price sitting on the 50, RSI under 60. Candidate dip. Stop is a close 2% under the 50. Prefer GROWTH if both show.

GROWTH

Do: this is the buy setup. Stricter dip: uptrend, on a rising 50, RSI 35–60, last days bouncing. Confirm on IBKR. Small size. Horizon is weeks, not 6–12 months.

LONG

Do: research the business, don’t buy the poke. Overlay on RECLAIM / WAIT / WATCH. Tape says 6–12 months is possible if earnings still look good and the 50 is later reclaimed. Not a bottom call. Clock is how long the 50 may take to catch the 200 at the recent slope — approximate.
