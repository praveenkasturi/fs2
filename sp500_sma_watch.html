AVOID

Do: skip. Below both lines and not even testing the near SMA — or a death cross. If it is walking into a line, the flag is still LEAVE TEST unless it is a real TEST with a GOOD TEST mark. First rallies usually fail.

Near death

Death cross

50 crossed below 200. Rallies often fail.

TEST

Do: watch the poke, don’t buy it. GOOD TEST / BREAK WATCH is the walk-up into the line: coming back after earlier fails, last days rising, within 5%, RSI mid, volume not exploding, OBV not dumping. Click BREAK WATCH to see them. Not a buy under the line — wait for a daily close through, then GAME ON.

Poke from below

Under both, tapping the near SMA. Do not buy the poke.

GAME ON

Do: must check. It already broke a SMA, then pulled back and is holding it as support. Then read BUY THE DIP vs LEAVE THE DIP — volume, RSI, OBV. Target is the next SMA. Stop is a close ~2% under the line it just won.

Retest holds

Broke the 50, came back, 50 held. Next ceiling is the 200.

RANGE

Do: sit. Price is between the 50 and the 200. One line is support, the other is the ceiling. It can chop here for days. No buy until it hugs the lower line (GAME ON) or breaks the upper one.

Between the lines

Price between the averages. Stall is the reverse.

CLEAR

Do: don’t chase the second break. Price is above both SMAs, but the 50 has not crossed the 200 yet. The second line just flipped to support. Wait for a retest (GAME ON) or a later RIZZ. Near a golden cross is still not a buy.

Repairing

Near golden

HOLD

Do: don’t chase. Full uptrend (50 already above 200) or a fresh golden cross. If you own it, you can hold. New money waits for the next dip to the 50.

RIZZ

How to read OBV

OBV is a running score of volume — up day adds, down day subtracts. Ignore the number. Compare the shape to price. This is a check, not a buy trigger. On a dip: still need a daily hold of the 50. On a TEST: still wait for a close through. Click BEST / OK / LEAVE to filter the table. Click again to clear.

Price dips

BEST

Price down, OBV up

Healthy dip. Selling is weaker than it looks. Watch the 50 hold.

OK

Price down, OBV flat

Money held. LMT-style. Fine if the 50 still holds.

LEAVE

Price down, OBV new low

Money left. Ugly dip or ugly test. Walk away.

Price rises

BEST

Price up, OBV up

Confirmed move. Buyers came with the rise.

OK

Price up, OBV flat

Soft rally. Don’t chase. Wait for a dip that holds.

LEAVE

Price up, OBV down

Weak rally. Price is up, money is leaving. Do not chase.
