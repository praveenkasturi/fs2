AVOID

Do: skip. Below both lines and not even testing the near SMA — or a death cross. If it is walking into a line, the flag is still LEAVE TEST unless it is a real TEST with a GOOD TEST mark. First rallies usually fail.

Downtrend

Price below both. Wait for a close above the 50.

Near death

50 over 200, close, not rising. Death nearby.

Death cross

50 crossed below 200. Rallies often fail.

TEST

Do: watch the poke, don’t buy it. GOOD TEST is the walk-up you want: coming back after earlier fails, last days rising, within ~4% of the line, RSI not crazy, volume not exploding. That is Friday Tesla into the 50. Anything else is LEAVE TEST. Still not a buy under the line — wait for a daily close through, then GAME ON on the hold.

Poke from below

Under both, tapping the near SMA. Do not buy the poke.

GAME ON

Do: must check. It already broke a SMA, then pulled back and is holding it as support. Then read BUY THE DIP vs LEAVE THE DIP — volume, RSI, OBV. Target is the next SMA. Stop is a close ~2% under the line it just won.

Retest holds

Broke the 50, came back, 50 held. Next ceiling is the 200.

RANGE

Do: sit. Price is between the 50 and the 200. One line is support, the other is the ceiling. It can chop here for days. No buy until it hugs the lower line (GAME ON) or breaks the upper one.

Between the lines

Price between the averages. Stall is the reverse.

CLEAR

Do: don’t chase the second break. Price is above both SMAs, but the 50 has not crossed the 200 yet. The second line just flipped to support. Wait for a retest (GAME ON) or a later RIZZ. Near a golden cross is still not a buy.

Repairing

Price above both. 50 has not crossed 200 yet.

Near golden

50 under 200, close, rising. Not a buy.

HOLD

Do: don’t chase. Full uptrend (50 already above 200) or a fresh golden cross. If you own it, you can hold. New money waits for the next dip to the 50.

Uptrend

Price above both. 50 already above 200.

Golden cross

50 crossed above 200. Prefer a dip to the 50.

RIZZ

Do: must check. Last stair: 50 is support in a finished uptrend. Then BUY THE DIP vs LEAVE THE DIP (volume dried up, RSI cooled, OBV held). Stop is a close 2% under the 50. Prefer GROWTH if both show.

Sit on the 50

Uptrend. Price came back to the 50. That is the dip.

GROWTH

Do: this is the cleanest dip. Uptrend, rising 50, RSI 35–60, last days bouncing. Confirm on IBKR. Small size. Weeks, not 6–12 months.

Dip plus bounce

Same dip, but last days are already turning up.

LONG

Do: research the business, don’t buy the poke. Overlay on TEST / RANGE / CLEAR. Tape says 6–12 months is possible if earnings still look good. Not a bottom call. This page has no earnings. No extra chart — LONG is not a stage.
